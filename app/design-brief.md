# MINTDEX design brief: Holo Soft

**Direction (user, most recent last):** "simple yet modern and elegant", "LOOK GREAT BUT NOT OVERLY COMPLICATED", "NO SCROLLING ONLY, LEFT TABS", "give the site more life and make the buttons and edges soft not sharp, i want a modern look", "add motion action animations and other site improvements", and "also please redesign the cards, the template is perfect, but the design is not consistent with the tier level, some high tier levels look ordinary and unspecial". Holo Soft is the restyle that answers the first five. It keeps the Viewing Room template and the Binder Stock layout (224px rail, 56px header row, fitting rules, FitList row heights, the no-scroll template at 1280x720, the mobile shell, copy and the CTA inventory) and changes the material, the shapes, the life cues and the motion. The card redesign (the sixth request) is its own step: it owns `MintCard.tsx` and every `.mdx-card*` rule.

Sources of truth, in order: `refs/soft-modern-delta.md` (Holo Soft: tokens, tints, type, recipes, motion, foil budget, route edits, QA), `refs/improvement-backlog.md` (honesty, performance, flows, phones, accessibility), then `refs/vintage-delta.md` and `refs/design-spec.md` for anything those two do not name. This brief is the summary every page must agree with.

**Design read:** Solana memecoin traders and collectors who want launching and trading a coin to feel like owning a one-of-one collectible. Register: calm, precise, soft and alive. Numbers must read at a glance, and buy, sell and bid must never be ambiguous.

**Concept spine:** the binder page becomes a soft, well lit object. The greyboard wall lightens to a warm pearl canvas, the full height columns float as paper panels with large radii, and every control is a pill or a soft rounded rectangle. One signature material ties it to the logo: the pastel foil of the Holo Seal, used only where it means something. The cards stay the most colorful things on screen, each in a tray whose corner is the card's own 1/8 in corner plus the gap, so the chrome repeats the card's shape. The vintage stays as a few large moments: Newsreader for the display line, page titles, the wordmark and the plates (No. 004, Roman numerals), and round dot leaders copied from the card's stat rows. Everything you work in is Instrument Sans, which is where "modern" is felt.

**House rules:**
1. Cards are the most colorful objects on every screen. Chrome color appears only as the foil (four meanings, below), tier tints that name a design, market green and red, and the sky arrival wash.
2. Soft, never sharp: every chrome corner comes from the radius scale. Nothing in the chrome is square, except dense table rows whose rule dividers must run straight (Coin recent trades).
3. Shadows belong to things that float: full height asides (rest), raised controls (raise), hover (lift), menus, popovers and toasts (float), dialogs (modal). Rows, chips, trays, inputs and content panels get a 1px ring at most. All shadows are warm (rgb 58 44 28), never black.
4. Green and red only mean the market and always come with a sign or a triangle. The single exception is the 6px live dot on two section headers.
5. Nothing scrolls on desktop at 1280x720 and up.
6. Serif names, sans works. Newsreader sets only `.mdx-display`, `.mdx-h1`, the wordmark and `.mdx-plate`. Never under 15px, never italic, never a control, row, table, stat, caption name or section title.
7. Every minted card sits in a tray concentric with its corner. The Launch preview is the one unsleeved card.
8. Motion answers something: a touch, a change of place, a new value, a new event, a load. Reduced motion turns all of it off with no lost information.

**Product mechanics (contract):**
- Launch: name, ticker, card art, description, optional website and X link, optional dev buy up to 20 SOL, 0.02 SOL launch fee. Every coin launches on pump.fun and its address ends in "mint". The curve mirrors pump.fun: 1B supply, 793.1M sold on the curve, virtual reserves 30 SOL / 1.073B tokens. When the curve sells out (about 85 SOL raised) the coin graduates to PumpSwap; test mode keeps trading on the same reserves.
- Card designs unlock with market cap (test rate 1 SOL = $150): Spark $0, Surge $15K, Nova $50K, Tide $100K, Apex $150K, Ember $200K, Bloom $300K, Mythic $400K, Aurora $500K, Relic $600K, Frost $700K, Regal $800K, Celestial $900K, Legend $1M. Fourteen designs, each with a Roman numeral (I to XIV). Every tier list, count, filter, slider, accent, ring and tint in the UI comes from TIERS / MAX_LEVEL in `src/lib/mintdex/tiers.ts`; never hard-code a tier color (the card redesign may change the accents, and the tints follow through `tintOf()`). Every card is exactly Pokemon card shaped: 63 x 88 mm (aspect 63/88) with 1/8 in corners (radius 5.04% / 3.61%), never stretched or cropped. A card keeps the best design it has reached for good (level never goes down; liveLevel is what the current market cap alone would unlock).
- The card: each coin has exactly one card, and whoever holds it is the owner. Trade fee is 1%: 0.5% fills the card's fee vault and 0.5% goes to the protocol. The owner withdraws the vault any time and edits the description. When the card sells, the outgoing owner keeps what is left in the vault.
- Card market: anyone bids (SOL held in escrow, minimum 0.1 SOL). The owner accepts any bid or lists the card for a fixed Buy now price. Every card sale pays a 2.5% protocol fee and a 5% royalty to the coin's launcher.
- Words: the glossary in `src/lib/mintdex/copy.ts` (card fees, fee vault, resale royalty, launcher, design, one-of-one) and the mode strings there (test mode now) decide every mode-dependent claim.
- Test economy: players enter with a handle and get 100 test SOL. Faucet of 50 test SOL when a wallet is under 5 SOL (6 hour cooldown). Real backend on D1 (+R2 for uploaded art).

**Palette (tokens in `src/mintdex.css`; names kept so every utility still works):**
- Surfaces: wall #E9E4DB (pearl canvas: content ground and rail ground), plinth #FAF8F4 (panel: asides, content panels, dialogs, menus, mobile bars), sheet #FFFEFC (fields, active rail pill, segmented thumb, secondary buttons, wallet card, labels on fills), tint #F1EDE6 (row hover on panel, chip buttons, Buy | Sell track, key hint), sleeve #F3EFE8 (the tray behind every minted card, nowhere else), wall-deep #DDD7CE (skeletons on the canvas), track rgb(58 44 28 / 0.07) (segmented tracks, select pills, ghost hover, progress and slider tracks), wash #DBEAF5 (arrival wash start color only).
- Ink and text: ink #1D1B18, ink hover #2B2722, slate #55514A (6.23 on canvas, 7.44 on panel), placeholder #6B665D (inside sheet fields), field #878075 (field borders, dashed dropzone, leader dots), hairline #D3CCC0 (chart grid, table header rule, ledge line), rule #ECE7DF (row dividers).
- Market: up #0B6340 (hover #0F6C45, soft #E7EEE9), down #A82A22 (hover #B5322A, soft #F6E9E6). Soft fills only behind the change pill.
- Foil stops (sampled from the Holo Seal): pink #F6C6DD, butter #F7EAC2, mint #C8F0DE, sky #C6E0F5, lilac #DCCDF5. `--holo` is their 125deg gradient, `--holo-flat` #EBD5EE the forced-colors and print fallback, `--sheen` the primary button sweep. `::selection` is ink on sky.
- Tier tints: each design's accent at 14% on sheet, stored as data (`tint` in tiers.ts, no `color-mix`). Ink on every tint is 13:1 or better. Tints appear only on TierTag chips and the Levels ladder indicator, never in menus or rows. Accents appear in the chrome only as the Coin design progress fill and the Levels slider fill, never as text. Ring colors appear only as the dashed chart threshold lines.
- Overlay rgb(29 27 24 / 0.32), no blur. Card shadow rgba(40, 31, 20, 0.3) (card-owned).

**Radius scale (roles, so they never collide with the shadcn aliases):** chip 6 ("/" key hint, tiny tags), row 12 (row hover, ladder rows and indicator, menu items, quick amount chips, chart tooltip, skeleton blocks), field 14 (inputs, rail tabs and rail pill, buttons inside `.mdx-pair` rows), pop 18 (menus, popovers, toasts, wallet card, dropzone), panel 24 (content panels, phone panels and dialogs, mobile tab bar top corners), aside 28 (full height asides, the Launch form panel, desktop dialogs), pill 999 (every button, segmented tracks and thumbs, Buy | Sell, select pills, rail search, chips, progress and slider tracks, stepper dots, avatars). Thumbs use round(size x 0.28). Frame minis 1.5px. Trays: `calc(5.04% + pocket) / calc(3.61% + pocket)`, concentric with the card corner.

**Shadows (six, warm):** ring (content panels, tray edge), rest (full height asides only), raise (active rail pill, segmented thumb, secondary buttons at rest, wallet card, slider thumb, chip hover), lift (button and wallet card hover), float (menus, popovers, toasts, chart tooltip), modal (dialogs). Rows, chips, trays, inputs, rail rows and content panels never get a drop shadow at rest.

**Asides:** the Home, Market and Levels asides and the Coin action column carry `.mdx-aside`. On short screens (height 820 or less, which covers 1280x720 and 1366x768) they run flush to the top, right and bottom with only their inner corners rounded; above 820px they float 12px off those edges with 28px corners, and their top padding is `calc(2rem - var(--gutter))` so the first heading keeps its y.

**Type:** Instrument Sans (variable, wght 400 to 700, wdth 75 to 100) for everything you click and read: body 15/1.5, small 13, micro 12, buttons 15/600, chips 13/500, section titles 16/600 (`.mdx-h2`), the caption name 26/600 (`.mdx-name`), dialog titles 18/600, empty state titles 17/600, guide step titles 16/600. Numerals at width 85, weight 500, tabular (`num`). Newsreader (re-cut to wght 500 to 700, opsz pinned) only for the Home display (clamp 36 to 54px, 500, -0.02em, balanced), page h1 (30, short 28, phones 26), the wordmark (22/600) and plates (15, guide numerals 20). Fragment Mono only for addresses. Two preloads: Instrument Sans latin and Newsreader latin. Inside the card nothing changes from the chrome side: the card pins its own faces.

**Motion (full table: `refs/design-spec.md` section 10, from delta section 7):**
- Tokens: ease-room (entrances, color, hover), ease-spring (about 6% overshoot: thumbs, rail pill, card lift, press release, dialogs, toasts), ease-exit (exits only), ease-sheen; durations press 100, fast 160, base 220, slow 300, progress 700, sheen 900, wash 1600, shimmer 1600; stagger 24ms capped at 10.
- Seventeen moments, no library: button hover lift and soft press; one primary sheen per hover (hover devices only); the rail pill springs to the active tab and its tile wears the foil; row hover fades; segmented and Buy | Sell thumbs slide; input focus halo; card lift 6px out of its tray; list entrances cascade on first mount and page turns (never on polls); route roots fade and rise 6px on mount (`.mdx-page`); new feed rows arrive with a sky wash and the section's live dot pings once (at most every 2s); watched hero numbers slide to their new real value; the chart endpoint pings on a new trade; tier progress and the Levels fills crossfade through the design colors; the Home featured step moves with its direction; menus, dialogs and toasts spring in and fade out.
- Wave 6 additions (backlog P0-2, P1-15 to P1-23), same tokens and rules: a 2px ink page-change line at the top of the content column while a page is still loading after 120ms (the target tab's foil waits at half strength, `main` is aria-busy); after a path change focus moves to the page h1 and the title is announced (search-only changes never move focus); live lists (Home activity, Coin trades, Market sales) hold still under the pointer, with focus inside, or while paused with the header's Pause button (kept per viewer), are logs that screen readers do not read row by row, and announce "2 new trades" politely; card shelves deal again with the stagger when the sort, design, search or tab changes; Discover sorts and filters on the client (instant); fitted grids lay out their server HTML from a viewport cookie; the Home featured card is a labeled carousel that also steps by swipe, announces "Honk, Mythic design, market cap $466K, 3 of 8" on a step only, and keeps focus on "View coin"; the chart line draws itself on mount and range changes (never on polls) and the endpoint springs in as it lands; a design unlocked while you watch fills the progress to the end in that design's color before it snaps to the next one, the design chip springs in, and the viewer's own unlocking buy lands the new design on the card together with the level toast.
- Rules: animate transform, opacity, color, background-color and box-shadow (plus the progress and segmented widths and the sheen's background-position). The only infinite animation is the skeleton shimmer while loading. No ambient idle loops (the Home card does not sway), no scroll effects, no view transitions, no FLIP feed slides, no rAF tweens. Entrances never hide server-rendered content waiting on JS. Never animate `.mdx-card` itself. Every JS moment checks `prefersReducedMotion()`; reduced motion makes every duration and delay 0, removes lifts, presses, sheens, washes, pings and the shimmer, and turns the Home step and the Levels crossfade into hard swaps.

**Foil budget (exactly four meanings):** (1) the logo, the Holo Seal in the rail and the mobile header; (2) where you are: the active rail tile, the active mobile tab pill, the active Home stepper dot (an 18x6 foil pill with a 1px ink ring); (3) level up: the rim and sheen on the level toast; (4) touch: the one-time sheen on a primary button. Flat foil stops also color avatars and the ok toast's check circle. Foil never carries meaning alone (1.13 to 1.41 against the panel): it always sits behind an ink glyph, an ink ring or a text label.

**Life cues (static color with a job, so the page reads alive when the feed is quiet):** 28px coin art thumbnails in activity rows; design names as tier tinted chips; Coin progress in the next design's accent with that design's frame mini waiting at the end; the Levels slider and ladder indicator recoloring through all fourteen designs; chart thresholds dashed in each design's ring color; pastel avatars; change pills (green or red tint) only in the Coin hero and the Home caption. Live without noise: the 6px live dot on "Live activity" (Home) and "Recent trades" (Coin), the sky wash on new rows, number ticks on watched hero values, the chart endpoint ping. Guardrails: no gradient backgrounds as decoration, no glass or blur, no glow, no neon, no purple haze.

**Devices:** trays (`.mdx-pocket`, 6px on shelves, 12px featured, phones 4 and 8; no layout change, no drop shadow, the card lifts out of it); the ledge (5px box, one faded hairline at its top, behind the trays); round dot leaders (`.mdx-leaders`, 2px dots at a 5px pitch) in the featured caption rows and the Coin quote list only; serif plates (`.mdx-plate`).

**Layout (user direction):** "templated simply, no scrolling, left tabs". Two zones on desktop and no top bar: a 224px rail on the canvas (Holo Seal lockup, pill search, six tabs with one sliding sheet pill, How MintDex works, wallet card) and the content area. Every view uses one template: a 56px page header row (serif title and one context line on the left, view controls and pager on the right), then a body in one of three families: stage + aside, shelf grid, or the Coin page's three columns. Every desktop view fits one screen at 1280x720 and up using fit-to-height lists, viewport-fitted card grids and pagers; 1024 to 1279 wide may scroll as a fallback. Phones (under 1024) get a 56px canvas header, a rounded bottom tab bar with a foil pill behind the active icon, 24px-corner panels inside the 16px gutter, and natural scrolling (tabs, strips and sheets preferred over longer stacks). Phone patterns (backlog wave 5, which supersedes the delta's cut of bottom sheets): every dialog and the Discover design menu and Test mode explainer open as a bottom sheet (components/mintdex/sheet.tsx on vaul: grip, swipe down, 44px close, CTA pinned in `.mdx-sheet-foot`, no autofocus on touch); the header has one-tap search (/discover?focus=1), an account sheet (wallet, Portfolio, faucet, guide, terms, Start over; the devnet wallet slot) or the guide icon and Enter MintDex; safe-area insets on every side, a static header and 44px tab bar when held sideways; the coin page is a compact hero (112px card, market cap, address) plus segmented tabs Trade | Card | Chart | Trades kept in ?tab=; Levels puts the designs in a snap strip under a height-fitted card with the slider below; Launch shows a 112px preview beside Name and Ticker. How MintDex works is three one-sentence steps with small pictures, linkable with ?guide=1, swiping sideways on phones.

**Tab plan:**
1. Home: headline "Every coin is a card." + Launch a coin (primary) / Browse the dex (ghost); one large featured card in its tray on the ledge with a caption (serif No., sans name, tier chip, leader rows for market cap with a change pill, Buy now and Top bid, View coin) and a dot stepper (one featured card per design, rarest first). Aside: Trending (top 5) and Live activity with the live dot and coin thumbnails.
2. Discover: Design menu and Sort select pills, a viewport-fitted shelf grid of cards in trays that cascade in, pager.
3. Launch: the form in a 28px panel beside the unsleeved live preview card.
4. Market: Listed / Top bids / All cards segmented tabs over a shelf grid; aside with three plain facts and Recent card sales.
5. Levels: "Same coin. Fourteen designs."; the card in its tray crossfading through the designs, the slider filled in the current design's accent; the ladder aside with serif numerals I to XIV and a tinted indicator that springs between rows; Play the climb.
6. Portfolio: @handle, stats strip, segmented tabs Coins / Cards / My bids / Offers, rounded ledger panels or a shelf grid, pager.
Coin page: object column (card in its tray, serif No., tier chip, design progress, address, links) | market column (serif name, hero market cap with a tick and a change pill, stats, one rounded panel with the chart and recent trades) | action column aside (Buy | Sell ticket with chip quick amounts and leader quote rows, then the card: owner, fee vault, Buy now, bids, a paired bid input and button).

**CTA inventory (one label per intent; every button is a pill, 44 / 32 / 48px; Accept 28px):**
- Primary (ink fill, sheet label, one sheen per hover): Launch a coin, Mint and launch, Enter MintDex, Place bid, Raise bid, Buy card, List, Update, Save description, Accept.
- Secondary (`variant="outline"`: sheet fill, faint ink ring, raise shadow): Delist, Cancel bid (row), Play the climb, Claim 50 test SOL, Place bid when a Buy now price is shown.
- Ghost (text button with a track pill on hover): Browse the dex on Home.
- Chip (12px soft rectangle on tint): the Coin quick amounts.
- Trade submit (48px, Up or Down fill, sheet label): Buy $TICKER, Sell $TICKER, with explicit states Enter to trade, Enter an amount, Not enough SOL, More than you hold, Filling...
- A button beside an input (`.mdx-pair`) takes the 14px field radius.
- Text links (ink 500, underline on hover, no arrows or brackets): See all, View coin, View all, How MintDex works, Withdraw, Edit description, Clear, Copy, and the error page's Browse the dex.

**Retired:** square chrome (Binder Stock's "round means collectible" rule), the masthead rule, the ink rail bar, underline tabs, the printed double rule ledge, serif section titles and caption names, `--shadow-pop`, the static-skeleton and no-motion rules of the old spec section 10.

**Avoided tells (all rounds):** warm cream + serif + terracotta, near-black + single acid accent, broadsheet hairline layout, SaaS card kit (identical rounded cards with grey drop shadows, gradient washes), glass and glow, tracked all-caps eyebrows, middle-dot meta strings, mono for every small label, arrows appended to links, purple haze.
