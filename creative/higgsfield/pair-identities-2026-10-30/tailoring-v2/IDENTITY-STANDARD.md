# Meme card identity standard

The user requested: “make each card design unique and tailored to the identity of the MEME.”

Keep the shared Launch a Card content structure. Name, creator artwork, pair strip and description stay in their canonical positions. Decorative changes belong in the perimeter, material, gaps and reverse; they must not cover the creator’s image or introduce footer slots.

Choose the frame from the admitted meme’s actual identity: its pose, expression, props, visual language and recognizable materials. A color swap on the same royal frame is insufficient. For example, knit and denim suit CHILLGUY, impact cuts suit BONK, terminal glass suits the admitted ASCII Fartcoin identity, and carved timber with bat motifs suits TRIPLET. Preserve the exact source identity associated with the verified quote mint.

Use Higgsfield for decorative artwork. Review actual outputs before activating a complete front/back pair. Reject invented labels, numbers, reserved title plates on reverses, generic ornament that weakens the identity, and mascot images that invade a creator field. Exact intrinsic logo lettering is allowed by the user; incidental profile text, addresses and captions are not.

When a generated interpretation changes a difficult logo or character, use the verified original identity pixels with the reviewed Higgsfield frame/materials. Record source hashes, deterministic crops/scales/masks and the actual contributing Higgsfield job IDs. Describe this as source composition, not a new generation. Keep limited source resolution explicit instead of claiming invented detail is a faithful recovery.

Preserve original masters and prior immutable NFT artwork. Activate only complete reviewed pairs through the shared acceptance selector, with v2 whole-pair priority. Keep incomplete or revoked work unavailable; do not silently restore an older generic pair. Verify the final rounded edges, both faces, shimmer, full rotation and pair selection in desktop and phone viewports.

This art standard does not authorize new credit purchases, duplicate retries after unknown submissions, deployment, financial transactions or publishing.
