# Meme Tailoring v2

Prepared 62 identity-specific pairs / 124 faces from the 66 exact pinned meme-catalogue references. DOGE, PNUT, WIF and EACC remain unchanged by explicit parent direction.

The canonical content positions are shared. Decorative frame geometry is not. Each brief has a specific visual mechanism from the actual reference, exact identity exclusions, front/back material continuity and source hashes. The guides are plain geometric conditioning images, not public card artwork. All public replacement art must come from Higgsfield after the parent quality pilot passes.

No source acceptance, asset activation, generation, credit spend or deployment occurs in this plan.
