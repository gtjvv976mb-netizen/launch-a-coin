# Meme identity tailoring audit

Reviewed 45 accepted complete meme pairs against the pinned exact-mint references. This is a read-only art-direction audit; acceptance and installed artwork are unchanged.

Most reverses repeat the same oval laurel, four-point star and stone lattice. Many fronts change only color and corner cameos. The artwork generally preserves the correct identity, but that does not by itself make the surrounding design unique. EACC’s energy field, WIF’s knit and PNUT’s shell relief are stronger examples of source-specific material. Fartcoin also uses a more distinct mechanical rail structure.

Keep the common creator-art, name, type and description grid. Keep print surfaces light, edges thin and rounded, and leave the creator artwork unobscured. Derive details from the actual source; do not invent a setting from a token name.

## Five priorities — ten proposed faces

### 1. BIKETYSON

The front repeats the source bicycle figure only as small corner cameos. The reverse places the exact bicycle-body subject inside the same gold laurel oval, star and black stone lattice used by unrelated memes. The bicycle mechanics do not inform the frame.

Source evidence: Exact source is the BIKE TYSON meme: a muscular human body forms the bicycle frame between two wheels, retaining the original head, handlebars and saddle. Preserve this absurd structure, not a normal cyclist.

Front: Keep the four shared content apertures fixed and light. Build the exterior perimeter from polished bronze bicycle-tube rails, spoke-laced wheel arcs at the rounded corners, fine chain-link engraving and dark tire-rubber inset strips. Use the source head only as small faithful corner reliefs. Keep all wheel/spoke forms outside the creator-art and print surfaces. Remove the laurel, generic star and marble lattice.

Back: Preserve the exact reference head and muscular human-body bicycle silhouette, not a normal bicycle or cyclist as the hero. Surround it with a sculpted wheel-rim/spoke halo and an asymmetrical chain-path border, using bronze tubing and rubber insets matching the front. A subtle reference-derived asphalt texture can ground the subject; no royal wreath, invented text, extra limbs or new branding.

### 2. FONE

Source evidence: The exact reference is a seated small brown monkey looking down at a black smartphone, on a pale neutral background. The phone and focused pose distinguish this identity.

Back: Keep the exact seated monkey, face and downturned black phone from the pinned reference. Use a rounded rectangular dark-glass recess, thin bronze hardware-like rim and quiet screen reflections to frame the subject. Repeat the front’s narrow phone-button/bevel geometry; remove the royal laurel circle, star and marble lattice. Do not invent phone screen content.

Use quiet amber relief lighting to bring out the carving. No laurel, generic star, added text, invented room, street scene or shop signage.

Back: Preserve the exact long wooden humanoid, oversized eyes, smile and bat from the pinned reference. Use a quiet wood-relief field and matching front copper accents. No new character pose, extra limb, sauna/street/shop scene, signage or unrelated royal ornaments.
