# Pair-specific Higgsfield card collection

Owner brief, 30 October 2026: give SOL, BTC and other crypto pairs their own designs; make every meme/stock pair's design visibly match its own exact identity; use Higgsfield for all card artwork.

## Direction

Use each exact quote asset's verified mascot or emblem as the defining visual identity. A general premium finish, approximate character, unrelated luxury motif, industry icon, palette swap or new label does not satisfy this brief. Examples: Fartcoin gets its verified ASCII source identity rather than Ember's flame; Pnut gets the actual squirrel rather than Relic's compass; a Tesla reverse carries its verified emblem rather than a battery; NVIDIA carries its verified eye rather than a generic microchip. Treat source image references as identity authority, not as optional inspiration.

The front remains a reusable creator template. Small exact identity reliefs and related material/shape language belong in the perimeter, outside the creator's art and information apertures. The reverse gives the pair identity the full central composition. Preserve each reference's defining shape, face, pose and colors through the premium sculpted material treatment.

## Shared construction

- Thin 63:88 card with smooth, fully contained rounded corners; no slab, sleeve, protrusions, case, pedestal or surrounding scene.
- Front content surfaces: horizontal extent 10%-90%; name at5.5%-11.5%; creator art at15%-59%; type at61.5%-64.9%; details at67%-92.5%. Source renderer metrics remain authoritative.
- Shared content surfaces stay free of theme art. No duplicate token portrait placed on top of the creator image. No market-cap inscription, tier/theme titles, serial, footer slots, invented lettering or placeholder signs.
- The owner explicitly authorizes exact source logos including their lettering. No added theme names, captions, serials or invented lettering; never substitute an invented wordless company symbol.
- Both faces share palette, metal/enamel/paper materials, edge construction, light direction, ornament density and foil treatment. Text-free supporting ornament; clean native material continues through the bottom edge.
- Use the current approved card fronts/backs as the geometric/material quality references. Keep all48 existing design identities, saved launches and immutable media recoverable.
