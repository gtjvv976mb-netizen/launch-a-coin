# Site premium artwork brief (29 October 2026)

Owner request: "make the site classic but premium, USE HIGGSFIELD". The owner added credits for it on 29 October 2026.
Layout and motion follow mintdex.fun (owner choice); this artwork only dresses that format. Never reference the CardDex
concept (`website-concept.webp`).

## Shared brief (every prompt carries it)

- **Identity:** a private collector's showroom for one-of-one trading cards: classic, quiet, expensive.
- **Palette:** deep ink #14110d, walnut #1f1a14, antique gold #d4a64a, card red #9e2b25 (rare accent), ivory #f3ead6.
- **Materials:**
  - black-brown velvet;
  - hand-rubbed dark walnut;
  - fine-grain leather;
  - engraved antique gold leaf with soft wear (never chrome, neon or glossy plastic).
- **Light:** one warm overhead spotlight with a soft falloff, a heavy vignette and a fine film grain. Low bloom.
- **Never:**
  - text, letters, numbers, logos or cards;
  - people, objects or furniture in the centre;
  - neon, cyan, blue or purple;
  - sci-fi grids;
  - busy patterns behind content.

## Pieces

motion), places the art, and makes the web derivatives. Provenance is in `jobs-site-premium.json`.
