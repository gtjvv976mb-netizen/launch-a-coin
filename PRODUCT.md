# Launch a Card product

For token creators who want their pump.fun launch to have a collectible identity. Choose a permanent premium card design, add original token art and metadata, launch the token paired with its one-of-one creator NFT card, then track token trades and holdings. Launch a Card is the platform; creators launch their own tokens through the pump.fun wallet integration.

The catalog contains 48 fixed designs: eight classic-inspired trading-card finishes, 14 premium material designs, 20 limited-edition themes, and six dedicated stock-reference designs. Every design has a matching text-free Higgsfield front frame and reverse. All 48 share one canonical MintDex content template; illustration, name, ticker, description, statistics and footer positions are fixed across the collection. The creator's own token image and metadata occupy the card content; decorative artwork does not add theme names or substitute a mascot for that image.

Paired stock editions select reviewed tokenized-stock quotes: GameStop/GMEX, Tesla/TSLAX, NVIDIA/NVDAX, Apple/AAPLX, Strategy/MSTRX and Coinbase/COINX. Matching limited finishes also bind those assets; the AMC finish binds the reviewed AMC tokenized equity. DOGE, BONK and WIF finishes bind their reviewed Solana quote tokens. Other finishes can explicitly select any of 16 supported assets, including additional memecoins. Themes without a reviewed corresponding quote remain decorative finishes and do not invent a stock or meme pair.

The launch screen shows the creator's ticker against the exact quote symbol, with mint, issuer and terms available before creation. Changing a bound finish's quote requires confirming a compatible finish; creation is blocked while that choice is pending. Quote identity and design are permanent. Existing coins keep their stored quote and are never reinterpreted from their visual theme. Stock pairing does not make the creator token or card company equity.

The owner explicitly removed NFT sales, bids and royalties. No automatic card evolution or market-cap design unlocks. Token buy/sell trading remains. Existing MintDex touch rotation, both-face holo shimmer, 3D inspection and case presentation are retained.

Current scope: independent application, not publicly deployed. Default Launch is the creator-wallet mainnet flow, with a separate explicit Test demo. The official-SDK flow prepares and simulates the complete selected-quote token transaction, verifies finalization, then issues the separate immutable creator Core NFT after another wallet approval. Public asset/metadata attestation, wallet ownership proof, exact signing/relay checks, duplicate prevention and expiry-safe recovery are implemented. Production actions stay disabled without independent hosting, durable public assets and a configured mainnet RPC.

The catalogue and buy/sell experience remain clearly labelled simulation, with separate test balances for every quote and demo NFT records. Demo fees and portfolio values stay in their asset’s units; custom quotes have no invented SOL/USD conversion. Real wallet launches do not spend demo balances. No real transaction has been signed or sent by the assistant; the first creator-approved launch remains a release check after deployment. Inherited MintDex chain deployment and remote account settings remain inactive.
