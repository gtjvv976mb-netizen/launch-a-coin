# Launch a Card product

For token creators who want their pump.fun launch to have a collectible identity. Choose a permanent premium card design, add original token art and metadata, launch the token paired with its one-of-one creator NFT card, then track token trades and holdings. Launch a Card is the platform; creators launch their own tokens through the pump.fun wallet integration.

The catalog contains 48 fixed designs: seven classic-inspired trading-card finishes, 15 premium material designs, 20 limited-edition themes, and six dedicated stock-reference designs. Every design has a matching text-free Higgsfield front frame and reverse. All 48 share the Launch a Card v2 content template; illustration, name, ticker, description and statistics positions are fixed across the collection. Bottom platform/serial labels and both former footer slots are removed. The creator's own token image and metadata occupy the card content; decorative artwork does not add theme names or substitute a mascot for that image.

Paired stock editions select reviewed tokenized-stock quotes: GameStop/GMEX, Tesla/TSLAX, NVIDIA/NVDAX, Apple/AAPLX, Strategy/MSTRX and Coinbase/COINX. Matching limited finishes also bind those assets; the AMC finish binds the reviewed AMC tokenized equity. DOGE, BONK and WIF finishes bind their reviewed Solana quote tokens. Other finishes can explicitly select any of 25 supported assets, including additional memecoins and collector tokens. Themes without a reviewed corresponding quote remain decorative finishes and do not invent a stock or meme pair.

The supported meme/collector collection offers seven existing premium finishes in nine explicit combinations: Obsidian Archive/CARDS; Pokémon Classic and Prism/SV151 or CARDS; Jade Reliquary/TOAD; Imperial Gold/DOGE; Stadium Chrome/BONK; Couture Knit/WIF. TOAD is the separately identified The Toad Pepe token, not an ambiguous PEPE mint. CARDS and SV151 use a distinct collector-token label. These chooser presets do not retroactively restrict neutral designs or change historical SOL/stock cards. The owner requested only published supported pairs, so unlisted PEPE and ambiguous POKEMON candidates are excluded.

The next expansion adds ten presets: Ember/Fartcoin, Relic/Pnut, Tide/MOODENG, Neon Circuit/TROLL, Frost/PENGU, Mythic/GIGA, Celestial/SPX, Regal/TRUMP, Wildtype/USELESS and Aurora/CHILLGUY. The current total is 17 finishes, 19 combinations and 16 meme/collector quotes. Existing premium artwork is reused without claiming new mascot illustrations. SPX is explicitly identified as the Wormhole Solana representation of the SPX6900 memecoin, not a stock asset. Exact quote filters and exact-symbol search resolve shared finishes to the intended quote while preserving the preview identity until selection.

The launch screen shows the creator's ticker against the exact quote symbol, with mint, issuer and terms available before creation. Changing a bound finish's quote requires confirming a compatible finish; creation is blocked while that choice is pending. Quote identity and design are permanent. Existing coins keep their stored quote and are never reinterpreted from their visual theme. Stock pairing does not make the creator token or card company equity.

The stock collection opens on a matching stock finish and shows each stock reference beside its actual quote symbol. The Launch design browser stays inside the form, previews the creator's own art and preserves their draft and launch mode. Neutral finishes with a stock quote can optionally adopt a matching premium or limited finish; opening that recommendation does not change the design. The archive blocks its launch link while a quote/finish change awaits confirmation. This stock-pairing refinement and the v2 fronts remain in local preview at the owner's explicit request.

The owner explicitly removed NFT sales, bids and royalties. No automatic card evolution or market-cap design unlocks. Token buy/sell trading remains. Existing MintDex touch rotation, both-face holo shimmer, 3D inspection and case presentation are retained.

Current scope: an independently hosted application with the production creator-wallet integration enabled; see HANDOFF.md for the deployed version and unresolved regional canonical-domain access. Default Launch is the creator-wallet mainnet flow, with a separate explicit Test demo. The official-SDK flow prepares and simulates the complete selected-quote token transaction, verifies finalization, then issues the separate immutable creator Core NFT after another wallet approval. Public asset/metadata attestation, wallet ownership proof, exact signing/relay checks, duplicate prevention and expiry-safe recovery are implemented. Local mainnet actions remain disabled. The latest preview changes are not deployed.

The catalogue and buy/sell experience remain clearly labelled simulation, with separate test balances for every quote and demo NFT records. Demo fees and portfolio values stay in their asset’s units; custom quotes have no invented SOL/USD conversion. Real wallet launches do not spend demo balances. No real transaction has been signed or sent by the assistant; the first creator-operated issuance remains unverified. The inherited MintDex chain remains inactive and separate.
