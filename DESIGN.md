# CardDex design direction

Keep MintDex’s original application composition: a desktop navigation rail, compact phone header and bottom navigation, an atmospheric sky and activity ticker, a central rotating card on Home, a card preview beside the design library, preview beside the launch form, and the three-column token page with phone tabs. CardDex uses a vivid Pokémon trading-card-inspired palette: warm cream card paper, obsidian framing, yellow and gold foil, charcoal ink and restrained scarlet-red accents. The application chrome uses no blue; individual card artworks retain their chosen palettes. Preserve the original typography, rounded 3D slab, both-face shimmer and direct touch controls.

The premium collector finish uses obsidian outer sleeves, cream paper panels, double yellow-foil frame lines, gold glints and crisp warm-metal controls. Navigation provides the obsidian-and-gold frame while forms and token data remain readable on paper. The crest is a premium card and crown. The brand is a premium card-and-crown symbol without lettering; the home link retains an accessible CardDex label. The environment and card artwork support the collector’s gallery; no podium circles sit below cards.

48 permanent creator-selectable designs cover recognizable trading-card layouts, premium metal and foil frames, limited editions, and six stock-reference designs. Creators’ token identity remains the card content. Theme labels live in the surrounding selection UI, not as decorative card names. Stock tickers and pairs are design references, with no shares or equity backing implied. The selected design stays fixed through token trades.

Full motion is the default. Card turntables, continuous both-face light response, direct dragging and phone tilt make each collectible inspectable. Viewport entrances and gold control glints enhance the surrounding interface without hiding server-rendered content or animating every offscreen row. Full/Calm, reduced-motion preferences and document visibility control the enhancements. Animate composited transforms and opacity, clean up observers and running animations, and retain readable states without motion.

The product is a simple token plus creator-owned NFT trading card. Token buy/sell remains; NFT sales, listings, bids, royalties, fee vaults and automatic design evolution are absent. The current local simulation is labelled clearly and does not claim that real tokens or NFTs have been minted.

Tool routing: web-design-workflow for the implementation process; Taste for appropriate art direction, Impeccable for responsive critique and finishing, Emil for interaction craft, and Playwright for real desktop and phone browser checks. Figma is conditional on supplied design context or requested canvas work. Higgsfield assets are integrated only after their approved generation completes and the files have been verified locally.
